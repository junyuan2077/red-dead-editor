Dear Diary,

Today is very very hot, but my heart is hotter because of Li Ming. He is my desk-mate, and he is the most handsom boy in our Grade 7 class. His eyes are like two big black grapes in the sweet grape soup, so shiny and bright. When he look at me, my heart is playing deer-jumping, bump-bump-bump, it want to fly out of my neck! I think he is the bright sun, and I am only a small grass who cannot breathe.

In the afternoon, we have a basketbal match. Li Ming is very good at play basketbal. When he shoot the ball, all the girls screem so loud. I also want to screem, but I am too cow to do that, so I only screem in my heart. He ran very fast, and his sweat is shining like diamonds. He is so cool, really cool, super cool!

After school, I saw him walking home. I want to ride bycicle with him, and watch the sunset together. Just like Jay Chou sings in his famous song 'Simple Love' (简单爱): 'I want to hold your hand like this and not let go, can I simple simple love you?' But my English is so bad, I cannot tell him. If I talk to him, my face will become very red like a big tomato.

I must good good study, day day up, so that he can notice me. Tomorow, I will try to say 'Hello' to him first. Wish me good luck, dear diary!
